Gamepad: RT / LT pedals, left stick steer, A handbrake, X winch hook, RB / LB winch in / out, Y camera, B recover, d-pad: up range, down locks, left 4WD, right lights. Right stick looks around.

Steering wheel: its paddles shift gears (pulling one puts the box in manual; G back to automatic) and an H-pattern shifter picks the gear its stick is in. Paddles on other buttons: set them in the settings (Esc). A wheel that steers too little (one the browser takes for an Xbox pad): Esc, Calibrate steering, and turn it lock to lock.

Tips: open diffs send power to the wheel with the least grip. Lock them in ruts and rocks, unlock them to turn. Low range for crawling, momentum for mud. Spinning tyres dig. Air down for sand, mud and rock (and air back up for the road). Ford deep water slowly: the bow wave reaches the intake first.
