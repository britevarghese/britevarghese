Start lines you up and counts down; or drive to a start line (the flags on the map) and press J. Beat the par times for medal money: each medal level pays once, and every finish pays a little. Gamepad: RT / LT pedals, left stick steer, A handbrake, X winch hook, RB / LB winch in / out, Y camera, B recover, d-pad: up range, down locks, left 4WD, right lights. Right stick looks around.

Tips: open diffs send power to the wheel with the least grip. Lock them in ruts and rocks, unlock them to turn. Low range for crawling, momentum for mud. Spinning tyres dig. Air down for sand, mud and rock (and air back up for the road). Ford deep water slowly: the bow wave reaches the intake first.
