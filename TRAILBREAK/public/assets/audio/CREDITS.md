# Sound credits

Engine recordings from [Freesound](https://freesound.org), cut into seamless loops at six rpm points (on and off
throttle) for NIGHTSHIFT and reused here. The game crossfades them by rpm and throttle.

- Engine `v8_muscle` (Ranger, Hauler): "Idle and Rev - Engine.wav" by wikusv, https://freesound.org/s/232272/
  (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), trimmed.
- Engine `i6_bmw` (Mule): "BMWM3_01.wav" by ikbenraar, https://freesound.org/s/415276/
  (CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), trimmed.

All other sounds (tyres, gravel, mud, water, wind, knocks, the winch, birds) are synthesised in the browser.
