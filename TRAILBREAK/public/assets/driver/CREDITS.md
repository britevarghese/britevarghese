# The driver

Both models are from Sketchfab, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). They were converted for real-time use for NIGHTSHIFT (animations
removed, textures re-encoded as WebP, geometry compressed with meshoptimizer) and are posed by the game (seated, the
arms reaching the steering wheel, the fingers round its rim).

- The driver: "indian man" by madmak21199, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/0304e3ede6f346adbe92439042ee7758 (CC BY 4.0),
  modified.
- The driver's gloved hands in the cab view: "Hand With Gloves" by teenjust500,
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/5a6a434b8ec943ffacc581358781eecb (CC BY 4.0), modified.
