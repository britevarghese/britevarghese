# Ground textures

Photo-scanned ground materials, all CC0 (public domain), packed for the game by `tools/ground.py` (colour resized to
1024 px, normal and height to 512 px, WebP). Thanks to their authors:

The coast's grass, leaf litter, shingle, red mud and river stones, and:

| Layer | Source |
|---|---|
