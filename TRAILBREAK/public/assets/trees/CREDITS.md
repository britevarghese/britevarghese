# Tree models

The game's trees are converted (tools/trees.py: geometry normalised, textures resized to WebP) from these models on
Sketchfab, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Ashwood Valley

- pine: "Pine Tree" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/pine-tree-d45218a3fab349e5b1de040f29e7b6f9) by evolveduk
  (https://sketchfab.com/evolveduk)
- fir: "Fir tree" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fir-tree-3f39aa5485e94477a36b435f7a1a8b54) by evolveduk
- spruce: "Spruce" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/spruce-a50a5df3164246a5af97992cec33a143) by evolveduk
- birch: "Birch tree" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/birch-tree-aa842dffd9654d33b8b91170ce83c172) by evolveduk

The dead trees are the spruce's trunk and branches without its needles.

## Kerala Ghats

- coconut: "Coconut Palm" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/coconut-palm-26e787f2ff2e4c0fb004c3b0210805a3) by evolveduk
- jungle: "Jungle Tree" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/jungle-tree-46f83ec5f6c04abf9d509c1070f67d1e) by
  kobaltsecond6c7d6150917a4267 (https://sketchfab.com/kobaltsecond6c7d6150917a4267); converted with only two in five
  of its leaf cards, each made larger
- mango: "Oak tree" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/oak-tree-6468dd4d3eb240ef902b9057d9913606) by evolveduk, standing
  in for the mango and jack trees
- rubber: "Elm tree" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/elm-tree-f36443d3e59946bfa38b3f5cb90ea5fa) by evolveduk,
  standing in for the rubber trees
- banana: "Banana Plant" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/banana-plant-85695b82c7ba4b3497a663616cc3bf25) by evolveduk
- bamboo: "bamboo" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bamboo-a02bf0e3ffe44617ad49daf3cd94fe59) by evolveduk

## Lalghati Canyon

- acacia: "Acacia tree" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/acacia-tree-bb14c2bb679b4d0bb1c578a27e2ddabf) by evolveduk,
  standing in for the babul
