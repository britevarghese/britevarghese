# Tree models

The game's trees are converted (tools/trees.py: geometry normalised, textures resized to WebP) from these models by
evolveduk on Sketchfab, licensed under CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):

- pine: "Pine Tree" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/pine-tree-d45218a3fab349e5b1de040f29e7b6f9) by evolveduk
  (https://sketchfab.com/evolveduk)
- fir: "Fir tree" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/fir-tree-3f39aa5485e94477a36b435f7a1a8b54) by evolveduk
- spruce: "Spruce" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/spruce-a50a5df3164246a5af97992cec33a143) by evolveduk
- birch: "Birch tree" (https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/birch-tree-aa842dffd9654d33b8b91170ce83c172) by evolveduk

The dead trees are the spruce's trunk and branches without its needles.
