# Truck model credits

These trucks' bodies are real 3D models from [Sketchfab](https://sketchfab.com), licensed under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Each was converted for the game with `tools/cars.py` (its settings in
`tools/cars/`): turned to face the game's way and scaled to the real truck's wheelbase (and width), the wheels, axles,
shocks and brake discs taken off (the game fits its own, on its physics), one rim kept for the game's wheels, the
body's paint, glass and lamps given the game's own (so the paint can be any colour, the glass cracks and the lamps
light), and the textures resized to WebP.

- `thar.glb` (Mahindra Thar): "THAR 4X4" by MB_Mahesh, https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/thar-4x4-66ee970b18014c98a1750064b65657b0
  (https://sketchfab.com/MB_Mahesh); narrowed 8% to the Thar's real width, its static axles, shocks and discs removed.
- `wrangler.glb` (Jeep Wrangler): "Jeep Wrangler 3D Model" by Suyog modak,
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/jeep-wrangler-3d-model-531f120594d946be8cd9d87b5095f856 (https://sketchfab.com/Suyogmdk);
  narrowed 12.5% to the JK's real track, its brake discs, spare rim and ground disc removed.
- `bronco.glb` (Ford Bronco): "Bronco Car Animation (Red)" by neshallads,
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bronco-car-animation-red-0f5a7d85d82540ab9436527fb8bbe4f7 (https://sketchfab.com/neshallads);
  narrowed 2.5% to the Sasquatch Bronco's track, its springs, shocks, arms and rear axle removed, its rims cut from its
  wheels.
- `shepherd.glb` (Land Rover Defender 110): "Land Rover Defender 110" by meeww,
  https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/land-rover-defender-110-5773ec04488b46148816b5f7a9f3f44f (https://sketchfab.com/meeww);
  narrowed 7% to the 110's real track, its axles, springs, roof rack and a prop removed, the parts but its panels
  simplified.
