NOTHING SHOWS?

  1. Plug the pedals into the wheel first, then the wheel's USB straight into the PC (no hub or extension). Press any button on the wheel with this window in front.
  2. Nitho Drive Pro V200: on a PC its light is purple (D-Input) or orange (X-Input). Hold the HOME button about 3 seconds to switch between them; try each, pressing a button after each switch.
  3. Check Windows sees it: press Start, type joy.cpl (Set up USB game controllers), Enter. The wheel should be listed, and Properties should move as you turn it and press the pedals. If it isn't listed, it's the cable, the USB port or the wheel's mode, not the game.
  4. Close programs that take controllers over: Steam (or turn off Steam Input for generic controllers), DS4Windows, x360ce, HidHide, the wheel maker's tool. Then unplug the wheel and plug it back in.
  5. Use Chrome or Edge, and open the game in only one tab or window.
