LANGUAGE-MODEL SOLDIERS

AI ZONE

Play with an AI soldier commanded by a language model: it reads the battlefield every few seconds, gives itself orders (capture flags, follow you, attack, take cover) and answers you on the radio (T to chat).
The key is sent to this game server only to call the model during your match; it is kept in memory for the room's lifetime and never shown to other players. Each AI soldier makes about 12 requests per minute — this uses your provider credits. AI rooms are private (invite friends with the room code).
